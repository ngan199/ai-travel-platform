# Travel Ontology — Competency Questions

These questions define what the travel knowledge graph must be able to answer.
Every class, property, and relationship in the ontology must serve at least one question here.

---

## 1. Destination Discovery and Matching

- Which destinations suit a traveler with [travel_style] and [budget_tier]?
- Which destinations are best for [activity_category] e.g. diving, hiking, wildlife watching?
- Which destinations are suitable for [traveler_type] e.g. family, solo, couple?
- Which destinations are near to [destination]?
- Which destinations are in [region / country / continent]?
- What is the climate type of [destination]?
- What is the typical daily cost in [destination]?
- Which destinations are considered hidden gems in [region]?

---

## 2. Timing and Seasonality

- What is the best time to visit [destination]?
- Which months should be avoided in [destination] and why?
- What season is [month] in [destination]?
- Which months are the [season_type e.g. rainy / high] season in [destination]?
- Which destinations have good weather in [month]?
- What is the crowd level in [destination] during [season]?
- Which destinations are best visited in [month] for [activity]?

---

## 3. Activity and Experience Planning

- What activities are available in [destination]?
- Which activities suit [travel_style] in [destination]?
- Which activities are suitable for [traveler_type] in [destination]?
- Are there festivals or events in [destination] during [month]?
- What tours are available in [destination]?
- What is the duration and price of [activity] in [destination]?
- Which venues in [destination] are tourist attractions?
- Which hidden gem venues are in [destination]?
- What activities are near [venue]?

---

## 4. Accommodation

- What accommodation options are available in [destination]?
- What accommodation suits [budget_tier] in [destination]?
- What accommodation suits [travel_style] in [destination]?
- What is the price per night for [accommodation_type] in [destination]?
- Which hotels in [destination] have [amenity e.g. pool, wifi]?
- Which accommodation options suit [traveler_type] in [destination]?

---

## 5. Transport and Getting There

- How do I get from [destination_A] to [destination_B]?
- What transport modes are available between [destination_A] and [destination_B]?
- How long does it take to travel from [destination_A] to [destination_B] by [transport_mode]?
- What is the cost of travelling from [destination_A] to [destination_B]?
- What transport hubs serve [destination]?
- What regional transport is available within [destination]?
- Do I need to book transport in advance for [route]?

---

## 6. Practical Information

- What currency is used in [destination]?
- What languages are spoken in [destination]?
- Is English widely spoken in [destination]?
- Do I need a visa to visit [destination]?
- Is a visa on arrival available for [destination]?
- How long can I stay in [destination] on a visa?
- What vaccinations or entry requirements apply to [destination]?
- What are the local laws I should know about in [destination]?
- What cultural norms should I follow in [destination]?
- What are the top safety tips for [destination]?
- What is the dress code for visiting religious sites in [destination]?

---

## 7. Itinerary Construction

- Given [destination_list], [duration], [budget_tier], and [travel_style] — what is a recommended day-by-day itinerary?
- Which activities in [destination] are recommended to do together?
- What is the logical order to visit [destination_list] given transport connections?
- How many days should I spend in [destination] to cover [activity_list]?
- What accommodation is available for each stop in [itinerary]?
- What transport connects each stop in [itinerary]?
